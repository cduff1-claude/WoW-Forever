# WoW Forever addons

Copy each folder into `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.

Each addon's folder has its own `README.md` with the details.

- **[CustomBuffwatch](CustomBuffwatch/README.md)**: Buffwatch++ patched for Forever: watch group members' buffs and see when they expire.
- **[CustomDrashQuestCompleteSound](CustomDrashQuestCompleteSound/README.md)**: Plays a sound and shows a message when a quest is ready to turn in (based on Drash_QuestCompleteSound).
- **[CustomFormBars](CustomFormBars/README.md)**: Keeps the main action bar on the right page for each druid form, so it can't desync.
- **[CustomQuestTracker](CustomQuestTracker/README.md)**: Click the "Quests" line of the quest tracker to minimise it to that line; click again to restore it.
- **[CustomWeaponSwap](CustomWeaponSwap/README.md)**: Weapon swaps per equipment set: set up under the character screen, swap from a movable bar or with `/ws 1`, `/ws 2`.
- **[CustomXToLevel](CustomXToLevel/README.md)**: XToLevelNG patched for Forever: mobs, quests and dungeons needed until your next level.
- **[DruidFrame](DruidFrame/README.md)**: Druid resource display (health, mana, energy/rage, cast bar, combo points, Omen of Clarity glow) with a form-aware range bar, target, target-of-target and tank's-target frames with aggro borders, Mark of the Wild / Thorns reminders, shift-cost shading, energy and rage warnings and the 5-second rule.
- **[DruidSequence](DruidSequence/README.md)**: Windows of spell sequences that fill with colour as you gain the mana to cast them.
- **[LFGForeverExtras](LFGForeverExtras/README.md)**: Additions to LFG Forever: member zones and guilds on group tooltips, click a group to /who its members, and %dungeon% in Ask to Join.
